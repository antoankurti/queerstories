About the project

Force est de constater, notamment via les réseaux sociaux que la vie des personnes queer a gagné énormément en qualité. Néanmoins, pour beaucoup de milieux sociaux et dans beaucoup de régions dans le monde, la discrimination des personnes queer n'est pas terminée. C'est un voyage à Sao Paulo qui a donné la génèse à ce projet. En effet, le Bresil est un endroit que l'on croit ouvert et inclusif, mais en le visitant l'on se rend compte que la discrimination est plus présente que nos idées reçues : le patriarcat fait partie intégrante de la culture, les mendiants blancs sont quasi inexistants, les jobs précaires sont réservés aux personnes de couleur etc. bref une société dirigée par la les suprémacistes blancs.

This project is about LGBT life in Sao Paulo. It concerns the realization of photo portraits and micro videos for the interview. At the end, I will pout all the interviews together, and tell one story. I want to talk about it in a political, philosophical and social aspects.

WHAT SHOULD BE RESPECTED

The most important word to consider here is diversity and respect. Thus for, I will ask you to express your opinions and your feelings, but not to be insulting to others, even if it’s not your thing. I want you to tell me your ideas and opinions about the topics below, and questions asked. You can express difficulties that you are experiencing or the opposite.

About me

I studies the academy of fine arts in Belglock;ium as a video artist. I stopped practicing it because I got very engaged in LGBT community, and finished by working in politics. For me it’s very important that people can be themselves and live their life without being judged, discriminated or persecuted. I come from a country (Kosovo) where being gay is not accepted. And I didn’t accept myself for more than 30 years. Now, I use my work somehow as a therapy but also I wanna leave documents behind me for the future generations, and of course those who will see my work now.
