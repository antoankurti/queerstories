Your story

This is not an interview. It is a conversation about the city you live in, who you are inside it, what it costs and what it gives back. There are no right answers, no statistics, no conclusion drawn in advance.

The conversation takes whatever form feels right: a video, a photograph, a voice recording, a text you send days later once the words have settled. Each medium holds something the others cannot. We use all of them, without hierarchy.

Visibility is not a requirement. A face can be blurred, a voice altered, a name replaced. Participation is not the same as exposure. If discretion matters to you, we will find a way together before anything is recorded or published.

The themes

The city — politics & institutions — religion — community — memory — visibility & discretion — body & desire — sex work — health — digital life — future — queer joy.
